Eight areas to check

Policy review

Information collected

User Data (name, email, password, phone, DOB, gender, address), Usage Data (search queries, streaming history, playlists, device info, IP addresses, cookies), Payment and Purchase Data, Voice Data, Message Data, Precise Location Data, Age Check Data

Purposes

To provide the Spotify Service, improve personalized recommendations, diagnose and troubleshoot issues, develop new features and AI models (AI DJ, AI playlist), marketing/advertising, comply with legal obligations, fulfill contractual obligations, detect fraud, business planning, process payments, keep service secure, conduct research and surveys

Third-party sharing

Service providers (customer support, technical infrastructure, security, marketing, eligibility verification), Payment partners, Advertising partners (no data to users under 18), Marketing Partners (no data to users under 18 by default), Ticketing and event partners, Podcast Hosting Platforms, Academic researchers (pseudonymized), Other Spotify group companies, Purchasers of our business, Courts and Authorities

Ads & profiling

Tailored advertising uses information to personalize ads (interest-based/targeted advertising). You can control tailored advertising through the 'Tailored Ads' setting on your account, click 'Your Privacy Choices' link at footer if not logged in, use podcast episode controls, or use browser with GPC signal enabled. Opt-out results in less relevant ads based on registration info and real-time usage. Younger users have tailored advertising turned off by default.

Retention

Data retained until you remove it. Search queries deleted after 90 days. Username, profile info, and streaming history retained until account deletion (then deleted or de-identified). Some data kept for extended time for legal/contractual obligations, investigations, or user safety. Age Check Data deleted immediately after check.

International transfers

Spotify transfers personal data internationally with Spotify group companies, subcontractors and partners. Uses tools to ensure compliance with applicable law, including technical protections such as encryption and pseudonymization, and policies to challenge disproportionate or unlawful government authority requests.

Security practices

Appropriate technical and organizational measures. Safeguards include pseudonymization, encryption, access and retention policies. Users encouraged to use strong password, never share password, limit computer access, log out on shared devices, and use 'Sign out everywhere' function.

Rights & deletion

Rights to be informed, know/access (Download your data tool), correction (Edit profile), deletion (remove content, close account via support page, contact us), opt-out of tailored advertising, data portability, no automated decision making, withdrawal of consent. Metrics: 8.2M deletion requests complied in 2025 with 15.47-day avg response time. Account deletion available at support.spotify.com/article/close-account/

Privacy controls

Download your data
Use the 'Download your data' tool on your Account Privacy page to request a copy of your personal data, or contact us directly.

Delete account
Follow the steps on the support page at support.spotify.com/article/close-account/ to delete account data and close your account.
"follow the steps on our support page. This data includes your User Data, Usage Data and other data"
Opt-out of tailored advertising
Use the 'Tailored Ads' setting on your account, or click 'Your Privacy Choices' link at the website footer. You can also use browser with GPC signal or use podcast episode controls.
"You can control tailored advertising through the 'Tailored Ads' setting. If you do not have an account... click the 'Your Privacy Choices' link"
Edit profile / Correct data
Edit your User Data under 'Edit profile' in your account settings or contact us for other corrections.
"You can edit your User Data under 'Edit profile' in your account or by contacting us."
Remove content
Remove audio content you have saved by selecting the content and choosing to remove it (e.g., remove a track from playlist or artist from library).
"to remove audio content you have saved, select the relevant content and choose to remove it."
Social settings / Private session
In Desktop/Mobile settings, control 'Social' options: start Private session, choose whether to share listening with followers, show recently played artists, turn off messaging.
"On the 'Social' setting, you can: start a Private session, choose to share what you listen to"

How to request deletion

Guide only

This checklist summarizes policy instructions. DataTrail does not send a request or verify identity.

1
Close your Spotify account
Navigate to the Spotify support page for closing accounts, follow the on-screen instructions to delete your account data including User Data, Usage Data, and other data listed in Section 3.
"follow the steps on our support page at support.spotify.com/article/close-account/"
2
Remove individual content
Remove audio content by selecting the relevant content and choosing to remove it. Examples include removing a track from a playlist or removing an artist from your library.
"to remove audio content you have saved, select the relevant content and choose to remove it."
3
Contact for other deletion requests
For deletion requests beyond account closure or content removal, contact Spotify's Data Protection Officer at privacy@spotify.com or by mail.
"for any other deletion requests, you can contact us"
